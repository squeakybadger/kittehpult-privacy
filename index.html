Privacy Policy for Kittehpult

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Kittehpult is a game about flinging cats onto a stack. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play — in short: it stays on your device, and we don't collect it.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game keeps — your best score, settings, and the cats you design in the cat designer (their colours, painted markings and any voice recording you make for them) — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any server that receives this data, and we have no access to it.

On Android, the game also writes a copy of your designed cats to Downloads/Kittehpult on your device, so they survive the game being uninstalled. You can also export a backup file yourself from Options and share it wherever you choose; that file goes only where you send it.

Microphone

The cat designer can record a short clip of your own cat's meow to use in the game. The game asks for microphone permission only when you tap the record button. Recordings are saved on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. You can delete a recording, or the whole cat, at any time in the designer.

Advertising

Kittehpult shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad after a round or every few minutes of play, and an optional one you can choose to watch in exchange for an instant autokitteh. These are the only parts of the game that use the network.

To serve them, AdMob receives technical information from your device such as a device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and basic device and app details. That processing is Google's, described in the Google privacy & terms and the AdMob policies. We receive no personal information from it.

On iOS you are asked, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Kittehpult may use that identifier for tracking. Say no and ads still appear, just less targeted; you can change the answer later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, the equivalent control is Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Buying the one-off remove ads unlock stops the full-screen ads and the requests that go with them; the optional reward ad stays available, because you choose when to watch it. The purchase is handled by Apple or Google — we never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or personal information is required or requested.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs of our own.
  • Nothing you make, record or score is sent anywhere.

Children

Kittehpult is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads shown to all players are served by Google as described above.
